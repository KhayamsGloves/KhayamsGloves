Our journey

Engineering trust and quality through years of manufacturing experience

CEO’s Message

KHAYAMS GLOVES MFG Co. (PVT) LTD. is a third-generation glove manufacturer and exporter based in Sialkot, Pakistan. Since our establishment in 2005, we have remained committed to quality craftsmanship, reliable production, and long-term customer relationships.

Our focus extends beyond manufacturing to include continuous product development, strict quality control, and adherence to international standards. We strive to meet our customers’ evolving requirements through dependable service, flexible manufacturing solutions, and professional support.

We welcome opportunities to collaborate with international brands and businesses, develop new glove products, and build lasting partnerships based on trust and mutual growth.
